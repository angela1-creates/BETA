# Design: Belay Partner Matching and Session Retention

Generated by `/office-hours` on 2026-11-17  
Branch: `feature/crew-beta-pilot`  
Status: APPROVED  
Mode: Startup

## Problem Statement

Top-rope climbers sometimes arrive without a partner, fail to find one through a front-desk broadcast, and leave without climbing. Existing group-chat and social-group workflows can find partners for later sessions, but messages are easy to miss, matching is slow, and the channel does not reliably connect people at the same gym and time.

BETA should turn a climber's availability into a mutually confirmed session and bring both climbers back at the right moments without requiring them to repeatedly check the app.

The first product promise is not "meet more climbers." It is: **know before you go that someone compatible will be there to climb with you.**

## Demand Evidence

- The founder personally experienced the failed workflow: a front-desk broadcast produced no match, the climbing session ended early, and the experience felt upsetting.
- A Little Red Book climbing group produced a partner only days later for a future session. This demonstrates that people will ask a community for help, but the current workflow is delayed and unreliable.
- Other climbing groups use unstructured date/time/location pings. Messages can be missed and matching rates are low.
- The gym owner expressed interest in BETA's belay-partner-finding feature.
- Reported Reddit discussions describe the same failure and connect repeated inability to find partners with membership non-renewal. This is supporting evidence, not yet verified BETA demand.

The evidence supports a pilot. It does not yet prove that enough compatible climbers will participate at the same gym and time to create a liquid marketplace.

## Status Quo

1. A solo climber arrives or plans a visit.
2. They ask the front desk to broadcast for a partner, or post in a social group with a date, time, and location.
3. Potential partners must hear or notice the message, respond manually, and coordinate separately.
4. If nobody responds, the climber waits, changes plans, boulders instead, or leaves.

The real competitors are the front-desk announcement, group chat, arriving with family, and not climbing. KAYA is more relevant to beta content than to this coordination problem.

## Target User and Narrowest Wedge

Start with consenting adult top-rope climbers at one partner gym who:

- intend to climb within the next seven days;
- do not already have a reliable partner for that session;
- can state a gym and an arrival window; and
- opt into transactional SMS for this notification-focused pilot.

Do not begin with instant "I am at the gym now" rescue matching. That experience requires high concurrent density and will feel broken during a small pilot. Begin with planned sessions, then test same-day matching only after the pilot measures enough supply.

## Constraints

- Partner matching is safety-sensitive. Experience and qualifications remain self-reported until the gym provides a verification mechanism.
- Phone numbers must not be shown to other users. SMS must be optional, verified, transactional, and easy to disable.
- BETA remains the source of truth for invitations, session status, blocking, reporting, and notification preferences.
- A reminder cannot manufacture marketplace liquidity. The product must expose low supply honestly and offer useful near-miss options.
- Matching should optimize schedule and discipline compatibility before climbing grade. Belay partners do not need to project the same grade.
- The pilot should avoid public popularity scores, competitive leaderboards, and mechanics that pressure people to climb when injured or unavailable.

SMS remains optional in the eventual product. Opt-in SMS is an enrollment requirement for this specific pilot because the experiment is testing whether timely delivery improves matching. A later cohort should compare SMS with inbox-only or email delivery.

## Scope Boundary

| Phase | Included | Explicitly excluded |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot v0, four weeks | structured availability intake, manual overlap checks, explicit SMS consent, five approved templates, mutual confirmation, cancellation, reconfirmation, completion check, block/report intake, basic funnel log | automated ranking, direct chat, calendar synchronization, recurring availability, adjacent gyms, groups, reputation, rewards, beta reflections |
| Product v1, only after pilot gates pass | automated overlap matching, in-app inbox, server-sent SMS, multiple interests, atomic acceptance, basic calendar event, templated logistics, mutual save and repeat request | open-ended chat, public ratings, rewards, generalized social feed |
| Deferred | same-night rescue matching, recurring schedules, adjacent-gym and small-group discovery, durable calendar sync, reliability indicators, gym rewards, post-session beta exchange | n/a |

The product roadmap is not a prerequisite for Pilot v0. The pilot uses controlled manual operations to test liquidity and notification response before BETA builds delivery infrastructure or a generalized ranking system.

## Premises

1. The urgent job is preventing a planned climbing session from failing for lack of a partner, not increasing time spent inside BETA.
2. Inbox-only notifications repeat the missed-message failure of group chats because users must remember to reopen BETA.
3. SMS is appropriate for sparse, time-sensitive transactional events, while BETA's inbox must retain the full state and safety controls.
4. Calendar reminders become useful only after both people confirm; they do not replace match and invitation notifications.
5. Retention should come from successful sessions, trusted relationships, and easy repeats, not artificial streak pressure.

## Current Product Evaluation

### What the current implementation gets right

- Availability captures gym, start time, discipline, partner type, and a short message.
- The live board is gym-scoped and exposes only a limited adult-consented profile.
- Invitation acceptance creates the session and both memberships in one server transaction.
- Confirmed sessions are selected chronologically and can be downloaded as an `.ics` event.
- Blocking, reporting, expiry, and adult-only safeguards exist.

### Highest-impact gaps

1. **Passive discovery:** pending invitations appear only inside Find a Partner. There is no delivery channel that reaches an absent user.
2. **A pending invitation removes supply:** `send_partner_invitation` changes the availability from `active` to `pending`, so one unanswered invitation hides the request from everyone else.
3. **Rigid time input:** the form offers only three relative days and three fixed times. It cannot express an arrival window or flexible alternatives. Recurring availability is deferred until repeated demand is observed.
4. **No compatibility ranking:** experience is displayed but not used, and schedule overlap is not calculated.
5. **No near-match recovery:** zero results ends at "be the first." The user cannot subscribe for later matches, widen time, try an adjacent gym, or join a small group.
6. **Calendar is a manual download:** the event does not yet have a durable update/cancellation path.
7. **No session reliability loop:** there is no reconfirmation, cancellation flow, completion check, favorite-partner promotion, or one-tap repeat.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Inbox and Calendar Only

Keep invitations in BETA and improve the confirmed-session calendar action.

- Effort: Small
- Risk: Medium
- Completeness: 5/10
- Advantage: minimal new infrastructure and personal data.
- Failure: users who do not reopen BETA still miss invitations.

### Approach B: Inbox, Opt-in Transactional SMS, and Calendar

Keep BETA as the authoritative record, send time-sensitive SMS deep links, and use the native calendar after confirmation.

- Effort: Medium
- Risk: Medium
- Completeness: 9/10
- Advantage: reaches users when attention matters without fragmenting session state.
- Cost: requires verified phone consent, delivery operations, abuse controls, quiet hours, and SMS expense.

### Approach C: SMS-first Concierge

Let users submit availability and coordinate primarily through text messages, with a human or automated concierge brokering matches.

- Effort: Medium initially, Large operationally
- Risk: High
- Completeness: 7/10
- Advantage: tests willingness to respond without requiring repeated app visits.
- Failure: state, privacy, moderation, and safety controls become fragmented, while BETA itself gains little durable value.

## Recommended Approach

Use Approach B.

Validate Approach B with a constrained concierge pilot. Pilot v0 uses a human operator only for match detection and approved SMS delivery; BETA remains the system of record. This is not the rejected SMS-first product in Approach C, where coordination and state would live primarily in text messages.

### Matching model

Rank candidates using:

1. same gym;
2. overlapping availability of at least 60 minutes;
3. compatible discipline and partner need;
4. optional experience preference; and
5. prior mutually saved partner, when applicable.

Treat grade and experience as preferences, not hard gates. Keep belay qualification separate and clearly self-reported.

The canonical Product v1 workflow is:

1. An owner publishes an availability request.
2. A climber with an overlapping active request receives one match-opportunity notification.
3. That climber selects **Interested**, creating an interest without hiding or locking the owner's request.
4. The owner receives one invitation notification and accepts or declines.
5. Acceptance closes the request and all competing interests in one transaction, then creates the confirmed session.

During Pilot v0, the operator performs steps 2 and 3 manually. The operator first asks the candidate whether they are interested, then asks the request owner to accept or decline. Participant phone numbers are never shared.

Allow multiple expressions of interest on one availability. Do not hide the request after the first interest. When the owner accepts one person, close competing interests politely and atomically. An interest expires at the earlier of 12 hours after creation or two hours before the requested session. The availability expires at its stated end time.

For Product v1, acceptance must lock the availability row, confirm that it remains active, confirm that neither participant has a conflicting confirmed session, accept exactly one interest, close all competing interests, and create the session and memberships in one transaction. Concurrent acceptance attempts must yield one winner and a clear "this match was already taken" result.

### Matching rules for Product v1

Hard filters:

- same gym;
- both requests active and owned by different, mutually unblocked adults;
- at least one discipline in common;
- availability overlap of at least 60 minutes; and
- no already-confirmed overlapping session.

Ranking among eligible candidates:

- overlap duration: `min(40, (overlap_minutes - 60) / 120 * 40)` points, floored at 0 and capped at 40;
- start-time difference: `max(0, 25 - absolute_start_difference_minutes / 120 * 25)` points;
- exact partner-need category match, such as both selecting Belay partner: 15 points, otherwise 0;
- optional experience preference satisfied: 10 points, otherwise 0; and
- prior mutually saved partner: 10 points.

Tie-break by the soonest session start, then the oldest request. Return at most five candidates. Experience never overrides a schedule or discipline incompatibility.

For two active requests, the earlier-created request is the canonical owner request and the sorted pair of request IDs is the deduplication key. Notify the top three candidates concurrently. If none expresses interest within 30 minutes, notify candidates four and five. Do not notify the same request pair again unless one party materially changes the gym, discipline, or time window.

When no exact result exists, Pilot v0 and Product v1 offer:

- keep my active request open and notify me when a compatible climber appears;
- widen the time window.

The later roadmap can add:

- show an adjacent time at the same gym;
- show another compatible gym chosen by the user; or
- look for a small group instead of a single partner.

### Notification roles

| Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|
| BETA inbox | Authoritative invitation and session history, actions, safety controls, notification settings |
| SMS | Immediate attention for new compatible availability, received invitation, acceptance, change, cancellation, and imminent reconfirmation |
| Calendar | Durable reminders after mutual confirmation |

Suggested Product v1 transactional cadence:

- immediate: one match opportunity to an eligible candidate, or one invitation to the request owner after the candidate expresses interest;
- immediate: invitation accepted, declined, cancelled, or changed;
- after confirmation: one-tap Add to Calendar;
- 24 hours before: session reminder;
- 2-3 hours before: optional one-tap "Still climbing?" reconfirmation;
- immediate: cancellation or failed reconfirmation.

Suppress redundant messages for sessions created less than 24 hours before the start. Match opportunities are sent only for a user-created active request, so they remain transactional. Respect 9:00 PM-8:00 AM in the gym's local time; only an explicit same-day-alert preference may bypass quiet hours. Every SMS must identify BETA, include the gym/time, avoid sensitive profile details, and deep-link to the authenticated session page.

Notification rules:

| Event | Recipient | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| match opportunity | eligible candidate | once per candidate/request pair; do not also send an invitation message |
| interest created | request owner | once; deep-link to accept or decline |
| accepted | both climbers | once after the database transaction commits |
| declined or interest expired | interested candidate | once; do not reveal competing candidates |
| 24-hour reminder | both climbers | suppress if confirmed less than 24 hours before start |
| reconfirmation | both climbers | send three hours before start; response closes 90 minutes before start |
| reconfirmation at risk | confirmed partner | once when the other participant misses the deadline |
| change proposed/accepted/rejected/expired | affected climber | once per transition |
| cancellation | affected climber | immediate and exempt from normal frequency cap |

Both climbers must reconfirm. Silence does not automatically cancel; it marks the session **at risk** and alerts the other person. A participant can then keep or cancel the plan. Any time or gym change requires the other participant's approval; otherwise the original session remains until cancelled.

Use an idempotency key composed of event type, recipient, request/session ID, and template version. Retry one transient SMS failure after five minutes, then preserve the event in the BETA inbox, the only Product v1 fallback channel. Cap match and invitation messages at four per active availability per day; confirmations, changes, and cancellations are exempt.

If a session starts less than three hours after confirmation, skip reconfirmation and allow `confirmed -> completed`, `cancelled`, or `disputed` directly. A proposed gym or time change creates a `change_proposal` in `pending`, `accepted`, `rejected`, or `expired` state; the original session remains authoritative until acceptance. A new block closes pending interests immediately and cancels any future shared session. The blocked person receives only a generic "this match is no longer available" notice so the blocker is not exposed.

Phone requirements:

- explicit opt-in separate from research consent;
- one-time number verification;
- transactional-only purpose statement;
- per-event notification controls;
- STOP/unsubscribe handling and frequency caps;
- no phone-number exposure to a partner;
- provider credentials and sending logic only on the server.

### Calendar behavior

The existing `.ics` export is a good fallback. Improve it into a real lifecycle:

- stable event UID per session;
- correct end time and time zone;
- gym address and safe meeting instructions;
- replacement and cancellation messages using a stable UID and incremented `SEQUENCE`, after the pilot validates the need;
- Apple/Google calendar-friendly actions; and
- a link back to the BETA session page.

### Key interface states

**Availability created**

> Looking for a top-rope partner  
> Movement Sunnyvale - Tue 5:30-8:00 PM  
> No exact matches yet. We will text you when someone compatible joins.  
> `[Edit window]` `[Pause request]`

**Match notification destination**

**Confirmed session**

> `[Add to calendar]` `[Send a logistics update]` `[Cancel]`

For Product v1, logistics updates are limited to reviewed templates such as "I am at the front desk," "I am running 10 minutes late," and "I need to cancel." Open-ended direct messaging is deferred because it creates a much larger moderation and retention surface. Pilot v0 uses the cancellation flow only; other logistics remain out of scope.

## Retention and Motivation Design

The product should be habit-forming, not compulsive. The best retention event is a completed climbing session that would otherwise have been missed.

### Intrinsic motivation

- **Relatedness:** turn a stranger match into a trusted repeat partner or small crew.
- **Autonomy:** let climbers control time windows, match preferences, contact channels, and whether to save someone.
- **Competence:** after climbing, optionally record what each person practiced or learned without ranking bodies or styles.
- **Purpose:** show the concrete value, such as "You found a partner and kept Tuesday's climbing session."

### Extrinsic motivation

Use restrained signals that reinforce reliability rather than popularity:

- private milestones such as "3 sessions climbed together";
- a possible reliability indicator only after enough mutually confirmed sessions and a separate safety review;
- recognition for prompt reconfirmation and respectful cancellation;
- gym-sponsored rewards only for completed partner sessions if the gym wants to test them.

Do not include a reliability indicator in the pilot. It can be mistaken for a safety claim, can be gamed, and requires dispute handling. Also avoid public leaderboards, follower counts, swiping, appearance-based ranking, daily streaks, or penalties for rest days. Those mechanics distort a safety-sensitive activity and can make rejection feel worse.

### Magic moments

1. **Relief:** "Someone compatible is available for the session you expected to miss."
2. **Certainty:** "Confirmed. You have someone to climb with Tuesday."
3. **Trust:** both climbers mark that they climbed together and choose to save each other.
4. **Continuity:** one tap turns a successful session into another plan.
5. **Learning:** after a session, partners can optionally compare approaches to one climb without declaring a universally best beta.

The first two moments validate partner matching. The fifth can later connect matching to BETA's personalized-beta hypothesis without forcing beta content into the initial wedge.

### Repeat loop

```text
Successful match -> completed climb -> mutual save -> one-tap repeat
        ^                                             |
        +------------- trusted relationship ----------+
```

This is stronger than an artificial streak because the reward exists outside the app: the user gets to climb.

## State Model

Pilot v0 records these states manually. Product v1 enforces them server-side.

| Entity | States | Allowed transitions |
|---|---|---|
| availability | active, paused, matched, cancelled, expired | active -> paused/cancelled/matched/expired; paused -> active/cancelled/expired |
| interest | pending, accepted, declined, withdrawn, expired, closed | pending -> accepted/declined/withdrawn/expired/closed |
| session | confirmed, reconfirmation_pending, reconfirmed, at_risk, cancelled, completed, disputed | confirmed -> reconfirmation_pending/cancelled/completed/disputed; reconfirmation_pending -> reconfirmed/at_risk/cancelled; reconfirmed -> completed/cancelled/disputed; at_risk -> reconfirmed/cancelled/completed/disputed |
| change proposal | pending, accepted, rejected, expired | pending -> accepted/rejected/expired; only acceptance changes the authoritative session |
| report | received, acknowledged, investigating, resolved, referred | received -> acknowledged -> investigating -> resolved/referred |

An availability can create only one session in Product v1. Support for multiple non-overlapping sessions or recurring availability is deferred. Editing the gym, discipline, or time after any interest exists closes those interests and requires fresh matching. Minor note edits do not.

Important edge cases for Product v1:

- prevent duplicate interests for the same candidate and request;
- prevent one participant from confirming overlapping sessions;
- close competing interests atomically when one is accepted;
- store all timestamps in UTC and display them in the gym's IANA time zone, including daylight-saving transitions;
- allow an operator to cancel for gym closure without penalizing either person;
- treat provider delivery failure as notification failure, not invitation failure;
- reverify a phone number after an account-level number change and stop delivery immediately after opt-out;
- record conflicting completion answers as `disputed`, not completed.

## Pilot v0 Runbook

### Intake and consent

1. Gym staff offers the pilot only to adults seeking a top-rope partner.
2. The participant opens a BETA form, accepts the adult and research notices, provides gym, date, arrival window, discipline, experience preference, and a verified phone number, then separately opts into transactional SMS.
3. The participant acknowledges that BETA does not verify identity or belay competence and that normal gym and partner safety checks still apply.

### Manual operation

- One named pilot operator is accountable for the matching log and participant support.
- Operating hours are 9:00 AM-8:00 PM Pacific Time. The intake form states that requests outside those hours are processed the next morning.
- The operator checks new and changed availability at least every two hours during operating hours.
- A manual match requires the same gym, one shared discipline, and at least 60 minutes of overlap. Experience preference is a tie-breaker only.
- The operator sends only approved templates through an access-controlled business SMS tool. Personal phones and ordinary group chats are not used.
- The operator first asks the candidate to express interest, then asks the request owner to accept or decline. No match is confirmed until both explicitly agree.
- A pending interest expires after 12 hours or two hours before the session, whichever is earlier.
- Cancellation and safety templates are available at all times from the BETA session page; messages received outside operating hours state the support window.

Operator coverage hours and automated notification quiet hours are separate policies. The first pilot operator works 9:00 AM-8:00 PM Pacific Time. Notification quiet hours are calculated in the pilot gym's IANA time zone. Expansion to another time zone requires a new coverage schedule.

### Pilot v0 approved SMS templates

Each link contains an opaque, single-purpose token and displays no participant details before authentication.

1. **Candidate interest:** `BETA: A top-rope request overlaps your plan at {gym}, {date/time}. Interested? {link} Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.`
2. **Owner acceptance:** `BETA: {first_name} is interested in your {gym} climbing plan for {date/time}. Accept or decline: {link} Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.`
3. **Confirmation:** `BETA: Confirmed. You have a climbing partner at {gym}, {date/time}. Meet at the front desk and complete normal safety checks: {link} Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.`
4. **Reconfirmation:** `BETA: Still climbing at {gym} today at {time}? Confirm or cancel by {deadline}: {link} Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.`
5. **Cancellation/safety:** `BETA: Your {gym} session for {date/time} is cancelled. Review options or report a concern: {link} Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.`

The selected business SMS tool must support registered sending, delivery webhooks, synchronized STOP/HELP handling, role-restricted access, audit logs, number verification, deep links, retries, and exportable consent evidence. Verify these capabilities before enrollment rather than assuming all business-texting tools provide them.

### Pilot v0 system of record

- BETA's pilot database is authoritative for consent, availability, interests, confirmations, cancellation, reconfirmation, completion answers, blocks, and reports.
- The SMS provider is authoritative for carrier delivery state and STOP/HELP events. A provider STOP immediately overrides any stale BETA notification preference and must sync back to BETA.
- The operator queue is a work view and audit trail, not a second participant database. Operators update participant state through BETA, not by editing a separate spreadsheet.
- If the SMS tool and BETA disagree about a match or session, BETA wins. If they disagree about permission to send SMS, the most restrictive state wins.
- Delivery state includes `queued`, `delivered`, `delivery_unknown`, `failed`, and `opted_out`. `delivery_unknown` is reported separately and never counted as delivered.

### Pilot v0 minimum build checklist

Existing foundations:

- adult consent and account flow;
- gym-scoped availability and partner board;
- invitation acceptance and confirmed session creation;
- block/report actions; and
- `.ics` calendar download.

Required before enrollment:

- phone verification plus versioned SMS consent and STOP sync;
- flexible arrival-window intake;
- restricted operator match queue using BETA records;
- minimal authenticated deep links for interest, acceptance, decline, cancellation, and report;
- a session page available at all times for cancellation and safety actions;
- reconfirmation and completion questions;
- the five templates above, provider delivery ingestion, and the authoritative event log; and
- named operational and safety owners in the launch checklist.

If this checklist is too large for the four-week timeline, use operator-entered BETA state and unique authenticated action links, but do not move authoritative session state into the SMS tool.

### Safety and incident handling

- The gym remains responsible for its facility rules, belay checks, and emergency response. BETA is not an emergency channel.
- Blocking immediately prevents future matching between the pair. A report can be submitted without contacting the other participant.
- The named operator acknowledges a safety report within one business day, marks its status, preserves access-restricted evidence, and refers immediate facility or physical-safety concerns to the gym's designated manager.
- Imminent danger instructions direct the participant to gym staff or local emergency services, not to wait for BETA support.
- A no-show or disputed session does not create a public score during the pilot. Repeated patterns are reviewed privately before the participant can receive another match.
- The pilot does not claim identity, qualification, or background verification unless the gym implements and documents that process.

Before launch, the checklist must name the primary and backup pilot operators, safety lead, privacy/deletion owner, gym escalation manager, credentials owner, and after-hours contact. Enrollment cannot begin while any role or access credential is blank.

### Privacy and retention

- Phone numbers are visible only to the minimum authorized operator and SMS provider, never to matched climbers.
- Store consent version, consent timestamp, number-verification timestamp, opt-out timestamp, and template/event delivery metadata.
- Remove the phone number within 30 days after pilot exit or opt-out unless a documented safety hold applies. Retain de-identified funnel events for analysis.
- Retain notification metadata for 90 days, then aggregate or delete it. Retain access-restricted report evidence for 12 months after resolution so repeat patterns can be investigated, then delete it unless the safety lead and privacy owner jointly document a legal or safety hold. Only the privacy/deletion owner can execute deletion; every hold and deletion is logged.
- Do not place participant names, phone numbers, report text, or free-form notes in analytics events.

## Build Gates

- Automate matching only after Pilot v0 observes enough overlapping candidate pairs to evaluate conversion.
- Build production SMS delivery only if manual SMS produces prompt responses and participants describe it as useful rather than intrusive.
- Build one-tap repeat and mutual save only if completed pairs express repeat intent or arrange another session.
- Build calendar updates only if cancellations or time changes frequently leave participants with stale calendar events.
- Test same-night rescue matching only when one gym regularly has enough concurrent seekers for a credible result.
- Revisit personalized beta after partner matching demonstrates completed sessions; do not use beta content to inflate the first pilot's scope.

## Success Criteria

Use **completed partner-assisted climbing sessions per active seeker** as the north-star outcome, not app opens or time in product.

### Metric definitions

- **Valid availability:** an adult-consented request at the pilot gym, starting 2 hours to 7 days in the future, with a 60-180 minute window and one supported discipline, not withdrawn within five minutes as an obvious correction.
- **Compatible candidate exists:** another unblocked participant has at least 60 minutes of overlap at the same gym and one shared discipline.
- **Delivered:** the SMS provider returns a delivered status. A queued or accepted status is not delivered.
- **Viewed:** the intended signed-in participant opens the deep-linked BETA event. SMS link previews do not count.
- **Mutually confirmed:** both participants explicitly accept before the start time.
- **Reconfirmation eligible:** a mutually confirmed session created at least three hours before its start. Short-notice sessions skip this funnel step and its denominator.
- **Late cancellation:** cancellation less than four hours before the scheduled start.
- **Completed by both:** both participants answer yes to the completion check within 48 hours.
- **Disputed:** the two completion answers differ, or one participant files a no-show report.
- **Unresolved safety incident:** a report has no assigned owner or misses the one-business-day acknowledgement target. Incident frequency and response-process compliance are reported separately.

Pilot funnel:

1. availability created;
2. at least one compatible candidate exists;
3. match notification delivered;
4. invitation viewed;
5. invitation accepted;
6. both climbers reconfirm when confirmation occurred at least three hours before the start;
7. session completed;
8. partner saved or repeat session planned.

Directional pilot learning thresholds, not statistically powered targets:

- roughly 30% or more of valid availability posts produce a mutually confirmed session;
- roughly 70% or more of confirmed sessions are reported completed by both people;
- median invitation response time is under 30 minutes when a compatible candidate exists;
- roughly 25% or more of completed pairs report repeat intent or plan another session within 14 days;
- roughly 10% or fewer confirmed sessions end in a late cancellation or no-show;
- every safety report meets the acknowledgement target and has an assigned owner.

Report raw numerators and denominators plus interval estimates, not percentages alone. Freeze the repeat-intent cohort 14 days before the analysis date so every included pair has the full observation window. With 20-30 participants, treat all rates as directional evidence and pair them with interview findings.

Instrument notification delivery, deep-link open, response latency, reconfirmation, cancellation timing, completion, mutual save, and repeat plan. Do not infer completion merely from a calendar reminder or app open.

## Distribution Plan

Pilot at one gym with adult top-rope climbers. Recruit where the failed workflow already occurs:

- a front-desk QR code shown when someone requests a partner broadcast;
- a staff script: "No match yet? Add your time window and BETA can notify you if someone compatible joins";
- a message to the gym's existing member channels; and
- invitations to the existing climbing social group.

The gym-side pitch is retention evidence: measure how many otherwise-at-risk sessions become completed visits. Do not pitch BETA as another social feed.

## The Assignment

Run a four-week concierge pilot at one gym before building a generalized matching algorithm.

Recruit 20-30 adult top-rope climbers who have previously lacked a partner. Collect their gym and availability windows, manually notify compatible pairs using the approved SMS templates, and record the complete funnel through confirmed and completed sessions. The key question is not whether people like the concept. It is whether enough overlapping supply exists and whether a notification turns that overlap into a completed climb.
